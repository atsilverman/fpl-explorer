Difficulty model

The schedule page does not use FPL’s fixture-difficulty or team-strength fields. It creates venue-specific ranks from xG, goals, xGC, goals conceded and clean sheets, then blends expected and actual ranks.

Promoted clubs Coventry, Ipswich and Hull have no historical FFH row, so ranks 18–20 are provisional rather than measured.

Derived metrics

Counts depend on what “derived” means

Main table columns

Players: 28 displayed stat fields; two are explicitly derived—G+A and position-selected CBIT/R. Official xGI is season-total only; it is no longer approximated on unsupported splits.

Teams: 16 displayed metrics; three are derived—GD, Pts/GP and G/GP. Combined totals are also sums of home and away.

Analytical transformations

Per-90, per-£m, price-rate change, expected-vs-actual differences, DEFCON threshold status, attack/defence composite ranks, fixture edges and schedule scores are calculated outputs—not independent source statistics.

Precision used: “Exact” means full stored-value equality (abs(diff) < 1e-6). Values are already carried at 3 decimal places from both sources. Rounding both sides to 2 decimal places does not raise the match rate — xG stays 35.1% and xA stays 25.9%. Only rounding to 1 decimal place lifts them (to 70.9% and 76.4%). The remaining gaps are real model differences, not display rounding.

Accuracy limits

What this dataset cannot establish reliably

Player venue defence

No accurate home/away player xGC, goals conceded, clean sheets, saves, official xGI or defensive-action totals.

Exact DEFCON frequency

Season actions per 90 cannot reveal how many individual matches actually crossed a per-match threshold.

Player xCS

The FPL API exposes no player-level expected-clean-sheets metric to compare with actual clean sheets.

Promoted teams

No measured prior-season Premier League profile; their matchup ranks are fixed provisional assumptions.

Historical detail

No old gameweek-level history or reliable past club affiliation from the live API alone.

Match-level analysis

The current inputs cannot support rolling form, opponent-adjusted performance or per-fixture team analysis.
